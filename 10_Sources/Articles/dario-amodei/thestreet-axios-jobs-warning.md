# TheStreet: "Anthropic CEO makes shocking admission about AI"

## Citation

TheStreet (2025). *Anthropic CEO makes shocking admission about AI*. Secondary coverage of Amodei remarks to Axios and Fox News.

## One-line summary

Aggregation of Amodei's labour-displacement forecasts: up to half of entry-level white-collar jobs gone within five years, pushing US unemployment to ten to twenty percent.

## Key claims

- Up to 50% of entry-level white-collar jobs eliminated within five years (Axios).
- Projected US unemployment spike of 10% to 20%.
- Timing window: one to five years (Fox News).
- Most exposed industries: finance, consulting, law, and tech.
- AI acts as "a general labor substitute for humans", not a single-task replacement. This breaks the prior pattern of cross-sector labour reallocation.
- Adjacent data cited: Big Tech new-graduate hiring down nearly 50% from pre-pandemic; ~55,000 AI-related US layoffs in 2025 (CNBC); 11.7% of US labour market technically replaceable (MIT study); 40% of employees fear job loss to AI (up from 28% in 2024).

## Excerpts

> "Entry-level jobs will be replaced by AI systems. We may indeed have a serious employment crisis on our hands."

> "I would not be surprised if somewhere between one and five years we start to see big effects here."

> "The technology is not replacing a single job but acting as a general labor substitute for humans."
