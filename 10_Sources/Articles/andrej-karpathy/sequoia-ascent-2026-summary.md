# Sequoia Ascent 2026 Summary

## Citation

Karpathy, Andrej. "Sequoia Ascent 2026 Summary". karpathy.bearblog.dev, 30 April 2026. https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/sequoia-ascent-2026/.

## One-line summary

Karpathy recaps his Sequoia AI Ascent 2026 fireside chat, articulating the Software 3.0 paradigm where LLMs become programmable through the context window and human value shifts toward understanding and direction.

## Key claims

- Software 3.0 means the context window becomes "the main lever" for directing LLM behaviour, replacing explicit code and learned weights.
- December 2025 was an inflection point: generated code chunks became "larger, more coherent, and more reliable," enabling delegation of macro-level tasks.
- Traditional software automates what you can specify; LLMs automate what you can verify - explaining why coding and maths improve fastest.
- Jagged intelligence arises because model capabilities depend on verifiability, training attention, data coverage, and economic value.
- Chess improved in GPT-4 partly because "a large amount of chess data made it into the pretraining set," not purely from general capability gains.
- Professional teams need systematic oversight, testing, and evaluation frameworks to coordinate fallible agents while preserving quality.
- "You can outsource your thinking, but you can not outsource your understanding."

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Karpathy uses the menugen restaurant app as a paradigm case for Software 3.0: the application logic shrinks to near zero when a multimodal model directly transforms inputs into outputs.
- His verifiability formula makes explicit a principle that appears implicitly across his other essays and talks; this post names it as a capability predictor.
- His Tesla-era pattern of "spin the data engine loop" reappears here as agent iteration loops: deploy, observe failure, refine, repeat.
- He treats understanding as a durable human skill even as execution automates, reinforcing the educational mission behind Eureka Labs.
