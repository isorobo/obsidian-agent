# Auto-grading Decade-Old Hacker News Discussions with Hindsight

## Citation

Karpathy, Andrej. "Auto-grading Decade-Old Hacker News Discussions with Hindsight". karpathy.bearblog.dev, 10 December 2025. https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/auto-grade-hn/.

## One-line summary

Karpathy uses GPT-5.1 to retrospectively score the prescience of 2015 Hacker News discussions, arguing that cheap LLM hindsight analysis trains foresight and that all present-day writing will face similar future scrutiny.

## Key claims

- LLMs can grade decade-old online discussions by analysing them with full hindsight, identifying which commenters predicted future trends accurately.
- The project downloaded 930 article-comment combinations from December 2015 Hacker News front pages and submitted them to Claude for analysis.
- Running 31 days of 30 articles through GPT-5.1 Thinking cost approximately $58 for 930 queries.
- "Future LLMs are watching. Everything we do today might be scrutinised in great detail in the future because doing so will be 'free'."
- Top prescient commenters from December 2015 included pcwalton, tptacek, and paulmd.
- The exercise captures the OpenAI founding announcement and Theranos revelations as prominent moments from that period.

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Karpathy treats LLMs as tools for meta-cognition: the model is not only a producer but an evaluator, grader, and retrospective analyst.
- His observation that future scrutiny will be "free" extends his economic argument from the "Power to the People" essay: computation collapses the cost of intellectual labour, including the labour of retrospective evaluation.
- The project reflects his interest in using AI to close epistemic loops that were previously too expensive to close - a practical analogue of his "verifiability" framework.
