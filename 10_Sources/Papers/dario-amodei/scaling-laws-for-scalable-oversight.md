# Scaling Laws for Scalable Oversight

## Citation

Title located via Rivista AI secondary coverage. Primary arXiv reference to be verified on next ingest pass.

## One-line summary

Analytical paper on recursive-oversight control techniques that surveys expert p(doom) estimates for artificial superintelligence. Cites Amodei's ten-to-twenty percent figure as a datapoint.

## Key claims

- Proposes p_c(ASI) - the "Compton constant" for ASI - as the probability that a loss-of-control outcome occurs.
- Surveys expert estimates: Turing and Hinton high enough that loss of control is the default; LeCun negligibly small; Amodei approximately ten to twenty percent (Fortune, 2023).
- Notes current lack of expert consensus despite existing research on recursive oversight.
- Draws analogy to Arthur Compton's 1945 estimate of atmospheric ignition risk at Trinity as under 3×10^-5.

## Excerpts

> "Alan Turing and Geoffrey Hinton have estimated p to be high enough to render control loss the default ASI outcome. Others such as Yann LeCun have estimated negligibly small probabilities, while Dario Amodei has estimated p ~ 10% - 20% (Fortune, 2023)."
