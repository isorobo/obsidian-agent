# Predictability and Surprise in Large Generative Models (2022)

## Citation

Deep Ganguli et al. (2022). *Predictability and Surprise in Large Generative Models*. ACM FAccT 2022. https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.07785

## One-line summary

Argues large generative models are paradoxically predictable in aggregate (loss scales smoothly) yet surprising in specific capabilities (emergent skills appear sharply at scale), with policy implications for governance.

## Key claims

- Aggregate model performance follows smooth scaling laws and is therefore predictable in advance.
- Specific downstream capabilities emerge discontinuously at threshold scales, producing surprise even for the developers.
- This predictability-surprise duality complicates pre-deployment risk assessment and calls for staged release frameworks.
- The paper grounds Anthropic's case for Responsible Scaling Policies and pre-deployment evaluations.
