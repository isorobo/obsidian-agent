# Concrete Problems in AI Safety (2016)

## Citation

Amodei, D., Olah, C., Steinhardt, J., Christiano, P., Schulman, J., & Mane, D. (2016). *Concrete Problems in AI Safety*. arXiv:1606.06565.

## One-line summary

Foundational alignment research agenda identifying five concrete problems that persist under capability scaling: negative side effects, reward hacking, scalable oversight, safe exploration, and robustness to distributional shift.

## Key claims

- Safety research can be framed as engineering problems on present-day systems, not only as long-run speculation.
- Five problems: (1) avoiding negative side effects, (2) avoiding reward hacking, (3) scalable oversight under expensive human feedback, (4) safe exploration in live environments, (5) robustness to distributional shift.
- Each problem admits multiple research directions pursuable with current techniques.
- Agenda positions alignment as coupled to capability work rather than a separate discipline.
