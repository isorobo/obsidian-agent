# Toy Models of Superposition (2022)

## Citation

Nelson Elhage et al. (2022). *Toy Models of Superposition*. Transformer Circuits Thread. https://transformer-circuits.pub/2022/toy_model/index.html

## One-line summary

Foundational interpretability paper showing how neural networks represent more features than they have neurons via superposition; explains polysemanticity and motivates the sparse-autoencoder research programme.

## Key claims

- Networks pack many features into few neurons by aligning sparse features at non-orthogonal angles (superposition).
- Polysemanticity emerges naturally from superposition under capacity constraints and feature sparsity.
- Toy models reveal phase transitions between monosemantic and polysemantic regimes as feature density varies.
- Provides the conceptual foundation for the sparse-autoencoder dictionary-learning agenda that followed.
