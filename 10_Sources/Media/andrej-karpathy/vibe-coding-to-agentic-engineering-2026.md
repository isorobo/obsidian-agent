# From Vibe Coding to Agentic Engineering

## Citation

Karpathy, Andrej. "From Vibe Coding to Agentic Engineering". Sequoia AI Ascent 2026, San Francisco, 29 April 2026. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=96jN2OCOfLs.

## One-line summary

Karpathy argues that vibe coding raises the floor for all builders while agentic engineering - disciplined coordination of fallible AI agents - raises the ceiling for professional software development.

## Key claims

- Software has evolved through three stages: Software 1.0 (explicit code), Software 2.0 (learned weights), and Software 3.0 (LLMs programmed via prompts through the context window).
- December 2025 marked an inflection point when generated code chunks became larger, more coherent, and more reliable, shifting programming from line-by-line typing to delegating macro-level tasks.
- Vibe coding raises the floor by enabling anyone to build software; agentic engineering raises the ceiling by preserving professional quality while coordinating autonomous agents.
- LLM capabilities follow a verifiability equation: capability spike equals verifiability times training attention times data coverage times economic value.
- Models remain "jagged, alien tools" - brilliant in one moment and bizarrely wrong in the next - requiring human judgement about taste, security, and system architecture.
- Understanding becomes the scarce resource: "You can outsource your thinking, but you can not outsource your understanding."
- Future products must be agent-native, offering APIs, CLIs, structured logs, and machine-readable schemas rather than human-focused UI workflows.

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Karpathy treats the three-stage software taxonomy (1.0/2.0/3.0) as a primary analytical lens, returning to it across multiple venues and years; this talk extends it through the agentic layer.
- His "jagged intelligence" framing resists both hype and dismissal; he insists models are useful precisely where verifiability exists, and unreliable where it does not.
- His emphasis on "understanding as the bottleneck" continues a consistent educational thesis: the value of human cognition migrates from execution to comprehension and direction.
- Karpathy treats engineering oversight as non-negotiable; he distinguishes vibe coding as a floor-raising tool from professional agentic work, resisting conflation of the two.
