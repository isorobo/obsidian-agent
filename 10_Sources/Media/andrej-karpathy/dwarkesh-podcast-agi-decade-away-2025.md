# Andrej Karpathy: AGI is still a decade away

## Citation

Karpathy, Andrej. "AGI is still a decade away". Dwarkesh Podcast, 17 October 2025. https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/andrej-karpathy.

## One-line summary

Karpathy argues that AGI remains a decade out, that reinforcement learning has deep structural problems, and that AI's economic impact will blend into the long arc of automation rather than rupture it.

## Key claims

- AGI is still approximately a decade away; the phrase "the decade of agents" rather than "the year of agents" is more accurate for the current stage.
- LLMs exhibit specific cognitive deficits relative to human intelligence, including model collapse when trained on synthetic data and in-context learning that fails to persist across sessions.
- Reinforcement learning is the worst training method available, except for all the alternatives - useful precisely because verifiable reward signals are rare.
- Self-driving took so long not due to engineering failure but because the problems were tractable-but-difficult, requiring the full maturation of deep learning infrastructure.
- AGI's economic impact will blend into the 2% annual productivity growth trajectory of the past two centuries rather than appearing as a sudden rupture.

## Reveals about tendency of thought

- Karpathy's gradualism on AGI timing reflects his consistent rejection of discontinuous-takeoff framings in favour of engineering realism.
- His "ghost/spirit" metaphor, recurring across multiple appearances in 2025, signals a sustained effort to build a new vocabulary for AI that escapes both the animal-intelligence and the sci-fi-robot frames.
- The claim that AGI growth will blend into historical productivity trends reveals an economic worldview anchored in long-run data rather than near-term disruption narratives.
