# MOC Thinkers

Navigational index of the fifteen thinkers tracked in this vault. Each entry links to the person profile. One-line descriptions capture the thinker's distinctive contribution.

## Scope

Fifteen curated figures across frontier labs, academic research, safety, economics, policy, and contrarian critique. The list privileges depth over breadth. Expansion requires a new entry here and a populated profile.
