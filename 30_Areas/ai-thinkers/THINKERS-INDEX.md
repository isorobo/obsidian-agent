# Wiki AI Thinkers Index

## Alphabetical Index

### A

#### [[20_People/dario-amodei/profile|Dario Amodei]]
Co-founder and CEO of Anthropic who led GPT-2 and GPT-3 development at OpenAI. Advocates for scaling with careful alignment research, responsible-scaling policies, and mechanistic interpretability as the path to controllable powerful AI.

#### [[20_People/andrej-karpathy/profile|Andrej Karpathy]]
Founder of Eureka Labs (AI education company) and former Tesla Autopilot director who conceptualises LLMs as a new operating system. Treats capability growth as engineering-observable and emphasises education as the adoption bottleneck.

#### [[20_People/leopold-aschenbrenner/profile|Leopold Aschenbrenner]]
Former OpenAI Superalignment researcher and author of "Situational Awareness"; estimates AGI by 2027 and superintelligence by 2028–30. Frames the transition as a national-security event requiring a Manhattan-scale US project with secure clusters.

#### [[20_People/daron-acemoglu/profile|Daron Acemoglu]]
Nobel laureate in Economics (2024) and Institute Professor at MIT specialising in institutional economics. Sceptical of AI productivity claims; argues current innovation incentives skew toward "so-so" automation that displaces workers without raising productivity.

### B

#### [[20_People/yoshua-bengio/profile|Yoshua Bengio]]
Turing laureate and scientific director of Mila; lead author of the International AI Safety Report. Advocates "scientist AI" over agentic systems to avoid loss-of-control risk and calls for international coordination and mandatory evaluations.

#### [[20_People/erik-brynjolfsson/profile|Erik Brynjolfsson]]
Stanford economist studying AI's labour-market impact through a J-curve productivity model. Warns of the "Turing Trap"—building AI that imitates rather than augments humans—and advocates redirecting innovation toward complementary tools.

### C

#### [[20_People/paul-christiano/profile|Paul Christiano]]
Head of AI Safety at the US AI Safety Institute and former OpenAI alignment lead; authored the RLHF paper. Argues alignment is tractable through preference learning but the default outcome is bad without serious effort; emphasises evaluations and compute governance.

### H

#### [[20_People/demis-hassabis/profile|Demis Hassabis]]
CEO of Google DeepMind and 2024 Nobel laureate in Chemistry for AlphaFold; believes AI advances science foremost through domain-specific breakthroughs. Expects AGI within a decade via grounded world models rather than scaling alone.

#### [[20_People/geoffrey-hinton/profile|Geoffrey Hinton]]
Turing laureate who pioneered backpropagation and deep learning; now an x-risk advocate after departing Google. Revised timelines to five to twenty years for AGI; assigns non-trivial probability to extinction-scale outcomes.

#### [[20_People/helen-toner/profile|Helen Toner]]
Former OpenAI board member and policy researcher at Georgetown's CSET; treats AI timelines as politically loaded and rejects assumptions that alignment is solved. Advocates compute-threshold governance, third-party evaluations, and separation of safety oversight from commercial pressure.

### L

#### [[20_People/yann-lecun/profile|Yann LeCun]]
Meta's Chief AI Scientist, Turing laureate, and pioneer of convolutional networks; rejects the LLM path to AGI. Argues world models and energy-based planning (JEPA) are necessary; existential risk is overblown and open-source weights should be the default.

### M

#### [[20_People/gary-marcus/profile|Gary Marcus]]
Emeritus NYU cognitive scientist and critic of LLM-only approaches who founded Geometric Intelligence and Robust.AI. Argues scaling hits diminishing returns on reasoning; advocates neurosymbolic hybrids and expects a commercial correction.

### O

#### [[20_People/chris-olah/profile|Chris Olah]]
Co-founder of Anthropic's interpretability team, formerly led clarity research at OpenAI and founded Distill. Argues that mechanistic interpretability through conceptual microscopy is the only route to verifiable alignment.

### S

#### [[20_People/sam-altman/profile|Sam Altman]]
CEO of OpenAI since 2019 and former Y Combinator president; believes compute is the new oil and iterative deployment solves alignment. Expects AGI "within a few thousand days" and advocates federal licensing and IAEA-style international bodies.

#### [[20_People/ilya-sutskever/profile|Ilya Sutskever]]
Co-founder of Safe Superintelligence Inc and former Chief Scientist of OpenAI; a core believer in the scaling hypothesis. Argues alignment research must precede deployment, not follow it, to avoid existential risk.
