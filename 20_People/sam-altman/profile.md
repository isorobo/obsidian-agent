# Sam Altman

## One-line thesis

Compute is the new oil; whoever builds the largest general-purpose model wins the century; deployment iterates the alignment problem into tractability.

## Current role and affiliations

CEO of OpenAI since 2019. Former president of Y Combinator. Co-founder of Loopt.

## Stated positions

### On AGI timelines

"A few thousand days" from late 2024. Short and accelerating.

### On takeoff dynamics

Continuous; describes the transition as something that will happen quietly rather than with a bang.

### On alignment difficulty

Solvable through iterative deployment; the real world surfaces problems faster than lab research.

### On economic impact

Abundance; UBI-like redistribution; capital returns to labour through AI equity stakes.

### On policy and governance

Federal licensing for frontier labs; international agency modelled on IAEA.

## Intellectual trajectory

Stanford dropout to Loopt founder to YC president. OpenAI co-founder 2015; CEO 2019. Board ouster and reinstatement November 2023. Published "The Intelligence Age" September 2024.
