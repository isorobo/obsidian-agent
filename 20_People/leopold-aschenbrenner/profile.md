# Leopold Aschenbrenner

## One-line thesis

AGI arrives by 2027 through straightforward scaling plus algorithmic efficiency; the transition is a national-security event; the United States must build a Manhattan-scale project to reach superintelligence safely and first.

## Current role and affiliations

Founder of Situational Awareness (hedge fund focused on AGI). Former researcher at OpenAI's Superalignment team (dismissed April 2024).

## Stated positions

### On AGI timelines

2027 point estimate; derived from OOMs of effective compute and algorithmic progress.

### On takeoff dynamics

Rapid intelligence explosion between AGI and superintelligence, compressed into 2028 to 2030.

### On alignment

Superalignment hard and time-constrained; requires massive research investment.

### On policy and governance

Framing as national-security imperative; argues for government-led Manhattan-scale project with secure clusters.

## Intellectual trajectory

Columbia undergraduate at 15. Forethought Foundation. Future Fund. OpenAI Superalignment 2023 to 2024. "Situational Awareness: The Decade Ahead" (June 2024) as the defining public document.
