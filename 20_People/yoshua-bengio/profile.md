# Yoshua Bengio

## One-line thesis

Agentic AI carries existential risk; a non-agentic "scientist AI" paradigm can deliver benefits without the loss-of-control failure mode; coordinated international governance is necessary.

## Current role and affiliations

Full professor at Universite de Montreal. Scientific director of Mila. Lead author of the International AI Safety Report.

## Stated positions

### On AGI timelines

Short; plausible within five to twenty years.

### On takeoff dynamics

Fast takeoff non-negligible; warrants precaution.

### On alignment difficulty

Hard under agentic paradigms; tractable under scientist-AI framing.

### On economic impact

Governance required to prevent capital and power concentration.

### On policy and governance

International coordination; mandatory evaluations; precautionary principle.

## Intellectual trajectory

Neural probabilistic language models (2003), deep learning textbook with Goodfellow and Courville (2016). Turing Award 2018. Public safety advocacy from 2023. Scientist AI framework 2024 to 2025.
