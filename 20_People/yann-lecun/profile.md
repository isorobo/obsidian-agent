# Yann LeCun

## One-line thesis

Autoregressive LLMs are a distraction; real intelligence requires world models, energy-based planning, and objective-driven agents (JEPA); existential risk is overblown; open source wins.

## Current role and affiliations

Vice President and Chief AI Scientist at Meta. Silver Professor at NYU.

## Stated positions

### On AGI timelines

LLMs alone will not reach AGI; architectures need world-model planning; timelines measured in decades.

### On takeoff dynamics

No fast takeoff; capability grows with compute and architectural iterations.

### On alignment difficulty

Not existential; objective-driven architectures constrain agents by construction.

### On economic impact

Productivity-positive; displacement concerns overstated.

### On policy and governance

Opposes restrictive frontier regulation; open weights release as default.

## Intellectual trajectory

LeNet and convolutional networks at Bell Labs. Turing Award 2018. Meta FAIR founder-director. JEPA architecture advocacy from 2022. Llama open-weights release 2023 to 2024.
