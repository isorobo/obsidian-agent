# Geoffrey Hinton

## One-line thesis

The architectures he helped invent may surpass human intelligence within decades; the extinction risk is non-trivial; the field owes the public a frank accounting.

## Current role and affiliations

Emeritus professor at the University of Toronto. Co-founder of the Vector Institute. Departed Google in May 2023 to speak freely on AI risk.

## Stated positions

### On AGI timelines

Five to twenty years, revised downward from earlier estimates of thirty-plus.

### On takeoff dynamics

Rapid and possibly uncontrollable once models acquire agency and sub-goal reasoning.

### On alignment difficulty

Existential risk plausible; assigns non-trivial probability to extinction-scale outcomes.

### On economic impact

Labour displacement severe; inequality acceleration unless productivity gains redistributed.

### On policy and governance

Supports binding international agreements; compares AI to nuclear for governance purposes.

## Intellectual trajectory

Boltzmann machines (1985), backpropagation popularisation, contrastive divergence, capsule networks, distillation. Turing Award 2018 with LeCun and Bengio. Google Brain 2013 to 2023. Public x-risk advocacy from 2023.
