# Chris Olah

## One-line thesis

Neural networks are open to inspection if we build the right conceptual microscope; interpretability is not decorative; it is the only route to verifiable alignment.

## Current role and affiliations

Co-founder of Anthropic. Leads the interpretability team. Former OpenAI clarity team lead. Former Google Brain researcher. Founding editor of Distill.

## Stated positions

### On alignment difficulty

Mechanistic interpretability progresses from toy features to real circuits to scalable automation. Each stage delivers measurable outcomes.

### On safety

Interpretability underwrites every other safety technique. Without it, RLHF and evals are surface-level.

## Intellectual trajectory

Self-taught researcher. Distill.pub founder 2016. OpenAI 2018 to 2021. Anthropic co-founder 2021. Key works: "Zoom In" (2020), "Toy Models of Superposition" (2022), "Scaling Monosemanticity" (2024).
