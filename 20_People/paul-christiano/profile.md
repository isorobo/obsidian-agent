# Paul Christiano

## One-line thesis

Alignment is tractable through preference-learning and latent-knowledge elicitation, but the default trajectory without serious effort is bad; evaluations and compute governance buy the time needed.

## Current role and affiliations

Head of AI Safety at the US AI Safety Institute (NIST). Former founder of the Alignment Research Center. Former head of language-model alignment at OpenAI.

## Stated positions

### On AGI timelines

Median estimates in the 2040s, shortened from earlier views; public probabilities on disaster scenarios.

### On alignment difficulty

Non-trivial probability of bad default outcome; eliciting latent knowledge (ELK) is a central unsolved problem.

### On policy and governance

Mandatory pre-deployment evaluations; dangerous-capability testing; compute-threshold triggers.

## Intellectual trajectory

RLHF paper (2017) with Jan Leike and others. OpenAI alignment lead. ARC founder 2021; ARC Evals into METR 2023. US AISI head 2024.
