# Ilya Sutskever

## One-line thesis

The scaling hypothesis is correct; superintelligence is the project; alignment research must precede deployment, not follow it.

## Current role and affiliations

Co-founder of Safe Superintelligence Inc (SSI), founded June 2024. Former co-founder and Chief Scientist of OpenAI (2015 to 2024). Former Google Brain researcher.

## Stated positions

### On AGI timelines

Short timelines; treats superintelligence as the near-term engineering target.

### On takeoff dynamics

Rapid once capability thresholds cross.

### On alignment difficulty

Solvable only if alignment research keeps pace with capability; founded SSI on that premise.

## Intellectual trajectory

Hinton PhD student at Toronto. Co-author of AlexNet (2012), seq2seq (2014), GPT papers. Co-led Superalignment team at OpenAI 2023-2024. Departed May 2024 after board-ouster aftermath. Founded SSI June 2024.
