# Helen Toner

## One-line thesis

Forecasts about AI capability are politically loaded; good governance does not wait for certainty; institutional design for frontier labs must assume alignment is unsolved.

## Current role and affiliations

Director of Strategy and Foundational Research Grants at Georgetown's Center for Security and Emerging Technology (CSET). Former OpenAI board member (2021 to 2023).

## Stated positions

### On governance

Compute-threshold triggers; third-party evaluations; whistleblower protections; separation of safety oversight from commercial pressure.

### On board accountability

The OpenAI November 2023 board crisis revealed structural weaknesses in non-profit-over-commercial oversight.

## Intellectual trajectory

Open Philanthropy. CSET from 2019. OpenAI board 2021 to 2023. TED talk May 2024 on the OpenAI board decision. Co-host of "Rising Tide" podcast.
