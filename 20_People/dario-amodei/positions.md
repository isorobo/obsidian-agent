## Open contradictions and tensions

- **2016 vs 2024 alignment framing.** "Concrete Problems in AI Safety" (2016) treats alignment as an open research agenda; by 2024 Amodei frames it as tractable-with-effort and rejects binary framings. Trajectory captured in [[10_Sources/Papers/dario-amodei/concrete-problems-in-ai-safety|Concrete Problems in AI Safety]].
- **2024 essay vs 2025 media remarks on labour.** *Machines of Loving Grace* emphasises long-run comparative advantage and abundance. 2025 Axios and Fox News remarks emphasise near-term 10-20% unemployment. Tone shift worth tracking.
- **Open-release stance pre-Anthropic vs current tiered-access RSP model.** Source to be ingested.
- **"Powerful AI" term preference vs ordinary-language AGI usage in interviews.** Amodei dislikes "AGI" (Machines of Loving Grace) but uses it in interview dialogue (Dwarkesh, Lex).
