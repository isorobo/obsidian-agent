# Demis Hassabis

## One-line thesis

AI advances science; AlphaFold-class breakthroughs in biology, materials, and energy define the near-term payoff; AGI follows from grounded world models, not scale alone.

## Current role and affiliations

CEO of Google DeepMind. Co-founder of DeepMind (2010); acquired by Google in 2014. Former neuroscience researcher at UCL and Gatsby. Former games industry designer. Nobel laureate in Chemistry (2024) for AlphaFold.

## Stated positions

### On AGI timelines

Publicly expects AGI within a decade from 2024 to 2025.

### On takeoff dynamics

Gradual, with discontinuities at specific scientific thresholds; AlphaFold-style jumps in discrete domains.

### On alignment difficulty

Solvable alongside capability; world-model grounding and task-specification rigour are core mitigations.

### On economic impact

AI-for-science dominates value creation; biology, materials, energy, and fusion are first to benefit.

### On policy and governance

International coordination body for frontier AI; pre-deployment evaluations; government access to models.

## Intellectual trajectory

Chess prodigy to games designer to neuroscience PhD to DeepMind founder. AlphaGo (2016), AlphaFold 2 (2020), AlphaFold 3 (2024), Gemini (2023). 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for protein structure prediction.
